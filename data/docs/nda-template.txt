Mutual NDA Template
===================

## Term

The confidentiality obligations in this agreement last for 3 years from the date it is signed.

## Permitted disclosure

A party may disclose confidential information to its professional advisers and to staff who need it, if they are bound by equivalent duties.

## Return of documents

On written request, each party will return or destroy the other party's confidential documents within 14 days and confirm this in writing.
