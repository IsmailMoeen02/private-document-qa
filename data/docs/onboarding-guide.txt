Onboarding Guide
================

## Your first day

Arrive at 9:30 and ask for the IT desk on the second floor to collect your laptop. Your manager will meet you at 10:00.

## Your buddy

Every new starter is paired with a buddy from another team for the first 3 months. Meet them in your first week.

## Training

Security awareness training must be completed within 7 days of starting. You will receive the link by email on day one.

## Pay

Salary is paid on the last working day of each month. The payroll cut-off for expenses and changes is the 20th of the month.
